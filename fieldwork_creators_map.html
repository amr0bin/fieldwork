The premise

Barriers to learning are design problems — best addressed when three commitments are treated as interdependent rather than separate. Every week leads with one of them; the other two support.

Accessibility — the floor, and who it still fails
Disability Justice — whose voice leads, nothing about us without us

What the reader walks away holding

Not a certificate — one accumulating research dossier: a thesis plus a tested, born-accessible learning intervention, told end to end. The evidence of a practitioner-researcher, returned to the community it came from.

What each week gives a peer in learning design

Three things every week: a concept or capacity to take on, a concrete move they can now make in their own work, and a real artifact they produce. Week by week the artifacts assemble into the dossier — the trail rolls up in four movements.

The value is a shift in stance: from consuming what the field hands down to investigating what is true in one's own context — accessibly, in a high-stakes setting — and returning that work to the people it serves.

The Discovery Pass — run first, every week

A twenty-minute pass that decides what a week is about before the template is applied. It sets five dials and, crucially, how hot to run each one for the week at hand — four techniques applied evenly would only be a new formula.
